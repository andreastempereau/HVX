Removal Impact:
===============

Size Reduction:
  Current size: ~500+ MB (includes venv, models, cache)
  After cleanup:
    - Remove .map_cache/: -50 MB (generated tiles)
    - Remove yolov4.weights: -45 MB (legacy model)
    - Remove services/: -5 MB (code)
    - Total savings: ~100 MB

Code Reduction:
  Current lines: 9,527
  After cleanup: ~7,400
  Reduction: ~22% smaller codebase

Process Simplification:
  FROM: 5 processes (video, perception, voice, orchestrator, ui)
  TO: 1 process (ui with all functions)
  Benefit: Simpler systemd config, lower overhead
